Privacy policy

Selia Speak, Android package com.majkeylab.seliaspeak, is published by Matěj Teplý, developer name Majkey. Contact: majkeylab@gmail.com.

The app has no account, advertising, analytics SDK or developer-operated cloud service. Speech recognition, optional language detection and optional text editing run on your phone using downloaded models. The app records only after you start dictation.

  • Accessibility: after your in-app agreement and Android permission, the app detects the keyboard and reads the active editable field, selection and window information. It uses these to show the microphone and insert or update your dictation. Fields reported as passwords, numeric or phone inputs are excluded. Some text editors omit their input type; these are supported when they expose an editable text control and text replacement. The app cannot infer an unreported numeric type. Password flags still block access. Field text stays on the phone. Changes made outside the current dictation stop further automatic insertion.
  • Audio and text: recording and recognition results are held in memory for processing, with a maximum recording duration of 15 minutes. The app does not save a dictation history or audio recordings. A short-lived Undo keeps the preceding field text in memory. Optional local editing processes the current dictation, not arbitrary earlier text from other apps.
  • Stored data: downloaded models, model choices, language routes, microphone position, onboarding and accessibility-consent settings remain in private app storage until you remove them or clear app data. App data is excluded from Android backup.
  • Downloads: model files are downloaded from Hugging Face over HTTPS and checked against pinned sizes and SHA-256 hashes. Hugging Face and its delivery providers receive your IP address and normal download request information. Dictation and field text are not uploaded. See Hugging Face privacy.
  • Clipboard: the app copies a result only when you choose Copy. It marks copied text as sensitive where Android supports that flag. Your system and other apps control clipboard retention and access.

Text inserted or copied into another app becomes subject to that app's handling. Only process recordings and text you are entitled to use.

Withdrawal, retention and deletion

Stop a recording with the floating control. Stop the app from its dashboard, revoke microphone permission, or disable Selia Speak in Android Accessibility settings to stop that access.

Remove downloaded models in the model browser. A selected model must first be deselected. To erase all models, settings and consent records, use Android Settings, Apps, Selia Speak, Storage, Clear storage, or uninstall the app. Text already inserted into another app and clipboard copies remain under that app's or Android's control. There is no Selia Speak account to delete.

The developer cannot remotely read or erase your phone. For support or a privacy request, email majkeylab@gmail.com. Do not include private recordings or text unless you choose to share them for support.

Your email address and message are received by the developer and email provider to handle your request. Correspondence is retained only while needed to resolve that request, a related dispute or a legal duty. You can contact the developer about access, correction or deletion, or contact a data protection authority such as the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection.

Terms and costs

Official Selia Speak builds are free to use. The app requires no API key, subscription or inference payment. Model downloads may use your network allowance. Processing consumes phone memory, battery and storage.

Review dictated and edited text before sending it. Speech recognition and AI editing can make mistakes. Supported languages, automatic detection and streaming depend on the selected model; catalog support does not guarantee accuracy or speed on every phone.
